Angular Statistics Meets New Galaxy Surveys

12–16 October 2026, Trieste, Italy

Scientific Rationale

The next generation of galaxy surveys will map the Universe with unprecedented precision. Realising their full scientific potential will require statistical tools that capture angular structure and information beyond conventional summary statistics. This focus week will bring together researchers working on galaxy surveys, cosmological inference, and angular statistics to compare methods, identify common challenges, and develop new collaborations.
